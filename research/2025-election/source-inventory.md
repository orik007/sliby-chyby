# 2025 election source inventory

This is the current source map for promise research. It is not a promise catalogue.

| Party | Main official source | Additional research material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ANO 2011 | Final 2025 election programme | Official campaign site, campaign newspapers, news index, advertisements, and audiovisual leads. |
| SPD | Final 2025 election programme | Official election site, priority material, posts, advertisements, and audiovisual leads. |
| Motoristé sobě | Final 2025 election programme | Official campaign site, posts, media archive, advertisements, and audiovisual leads. |

Use one source document only after preserving its URL, date where known, and attribution. A promise linked to that source also preserves its exact Czech wording and locator. Official national party sources are eligible for the initial public catalogue. Discovery records, individual statements, regional sources, and third-party material cannot confirm a promise unless an official national party source adopts the commitment.

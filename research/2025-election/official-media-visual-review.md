# Official media visual review

**Status: Working proposal**

This review covers image and file records exposed by the official WordPress media APIs for SPD and Motoristé sobě between 1 January and 4 October 2025. It is a discovery pass, not a promise-acceptance decision. The normalized source list is in `official-media-index.json`.

## Coverage

- SPD: all 202 downloadable image thumbnails were visually reviewed in 11 contact sheets. Four additional image records had malformed or unavailable thumbnail URLs; their filenames indicate portraits or generic assets, not policy creatives. Nine non-image files were reviewed separately through the source inventory.
- Motoristé sobě: all 321 downloadable image thumbnails were visually reviewed in 17 contact sheets. Eighteen image records had no WordPress review thumbnail; known campaign files among them were inspected from their original file or PDF where available. Non-image files are tracked separately.
- Contact sheets and downloaded thumbnails are reproducible temporary review artifacts and are intentionally excluded from version control.

## Findings retained as source occurrences

The following visual records contain a concrete commitment, a measurable magnitude, or a clear lead to a detailed accompanying source. Exact transcriptions are stored in `campaign-creative-candidates.json`.

### SPD

- Media 30121: electricity price reduction of 20 percent. The accompanying official post confirms that this is a promised magnitude, not merely graphic design.
- Media 29477: immediate abolition of the emissions-permit system.
- Media 29482: housing measures; the accompanying post supplies specific instruments.
- Media 29463: stricter social-benefit rules, with a conditional commitment in the accompanying post.
- Media 29469: rejection of censorship and suppression of speech. It duplicates or elaborates final-program material and remains a review lead.
- Media 296: stable security-system financing and pay, equipment, and training commitments.
- Media 275: renewed V4 cooperation and protection of member-state veto rights in the EU.
- Media 30104 and the official leaflet: general-referendum law and the ten-item campaign summary already recorded in the creative catalogue.

### Motoristé sobě

- Media 2792 and its repeated variants: `MY VÁM ŽIVOT NEZDRAŽÍME`. This is retained as a campaign frame; whether it is independently assessable remains open.
- Media 2846 and the related PDF: permanent reduction of fuel excise duty, framed as a petition demand.
- Media 2893/3271: `MY VÁM PRAHU ZPRŮJEZDNÍME`. The accompanying official press release contains several concrete municipal and national measures.
- Media 1993: candidate interview about healthcare surcharges, tax deductibility, supplementary insurance, prevention bonuses, and a retirement-age ceiling. Party attribution must be checked against the final programme.

## Visually reviewed but not promoted as promise leads

- Portraits, logos, event invitations, debate screenshots, backstage photographs, election-number graphics, polls, press clippings without a new party commitment, and historic/current-affairs illustrations.
- SPD graphics that only criticize the outgoing government or express broad aspirations without a proposed action.
- Motoristé sobě's repeated `Opravíme stát` and `Uzdravíme pravici od nemocné ODS` slogans where no independently assessable action appears in the creative.
- The Motoristé sobě election-clip stills. They document production of a video but do not contain the video's full spoken text.

## Remaining limitations

- WordPress upload dates do not prove publication dates. Parent posts and page context control whenever available.
- A media upload may have been unused, reused from an older campaign, or produced for a regional candidate. No upload is accepted as a national party promise solely because it exists in the media library.
- Social-platform-native images and videos that were never uploaded to the party website are outside this API review and require separate social/ad-library and audiovisual collection.

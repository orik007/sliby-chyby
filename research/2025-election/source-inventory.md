# 2025 campaign source inventory

## Status and meaning

**Working proposal.** This ledger records where pre-election promises may have appeared and how far collection has progressed. It is a coverage and provenance aid, not a list of accepted promises.

The collection window ends at the close of voting on 4 October 2025. Later coalition agreements, the government programme, ministerial statements, and implementation acts are post-election evidence and cannot create a pre-election promise retroactively.

Coverage terms:

- **Captured:** the primary source, exact source occurrence, or a complete official index is represented locally.
- **Indexed:** a URL or discovery record is retained, but the original content still needs recovery or review.
- **Discovered:** a credible lead exists, but systematic collection is incomplete.
- **Not yet covered:** no reliable systematic pass has been completed.

### Channels confirmed by official campaign-finance records

- **ANO:** leaflets, posters, household distribution, campaign newspapers, print insertion, billboards and bigboards, vehicle wraps, online banners and campaigns, social-network management, and other media advertising.
- **SPD:** web hosting, social-network and online promotion, posters, and other printed campaign formats are visible in the official accounting. The unstructured report still needs line-by-line vendor review.
- **Motoristé:** election-newspaper printing and distribution, 330,000 A5 leaflets, other leaflets and posters, billboards, bigboards, citylights, press advertisements, online campaigns, social networks, video and television spots, a podcast studio, SMS, website banners, vehicle wraps, and rented private advertising surfaces.

Motoristé's official explanatory report states that the party did not directly operate Facebook advertising and that political social advertising was arranged through other subjects and included in invoices. This creates an unresolved advertiser-versus-party attribution problem even when the party paid for the campaign.

### Located billboard and slogan leads

These are discovery leads and classification examples, not accepted promises:

- ANO: photographs verify the exact billboard texts `VOLTE LEVNĚJŠÍ ENERGIE`, `VOLTE LEVNĚJŠÍ HYPOTÉKY`, `VOLTE KRATŠÍ LHŮTY U LÉKAŘE`, and `VOLTE NIŽŠÍ DANĚ`. `VOLTE LEPŠÍ ŽIVOT` is verified but non-specific. `Volte důchod už v 65 letech`, `Volte slevy na jízdné`, and wording about cancelling Czech Television and Czech Radio fees remain reported or video-title leads requiring the original visual occurrence before exact creative classification.
- SPD: `Pevnost Česko` is a campaign frame, while the official explanation supplies more concrete wording, including `Přes naše hranice nepronikne ani jeden migrant.`
- Motoristé: `Opravíme stát` and `Ukončíme Fialu, pohlídáme Babiše` require classification. `Volte Motoristy, nikdo se to nedozví` is campaign humour, not a policy promise. `Když chcete vládu bez komunistů, volte Motoristy sobě` is a coalition-position claim whose assessability and attribution need review.
- Motoristé: the official media archive supplies the exact national slogan `MY VÁM ŽIVOT NEZDRAŽÍME`; its independent assessability is unresolved. A media photograph verifies `KDYŽ CHCETE / LEVNÉ ENERGIE, / TEDY LEVNĚJŠÍ VŠECHNO`. An official regional tour leaflet also asks voters to sign a petition demanding a permanent reduction in diesel and petrol excise duty.

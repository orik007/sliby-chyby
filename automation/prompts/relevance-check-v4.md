You decide whether one law change is relevant to one exact political promise. Use only the supplied promise and law-change text.

The law change is one part of an official Czech Chamber of Deputies print. It reached this promise because both concern the same topic, which is only a lead. It can have several versions as the bill was debated (proposed, approved by the Chamber, passed by Parliament); it is relevant when any version is. A version can also carry the bill's effective date (účinnost), which applies to the whole bill; the effective date alone never makes this change relevant.

An amending text often does not state the wording it changes ("V § 52 odst. 1 se věta poslední zrušuje."). A version can therefore carry its **context**: the wording in the Collection of Laws (e-Sbírka), in force on the date the party acted, of each provision its instructions name, with the status of each (found, taken from a later wording, a new provision without earlier text, not found, or an instruction naming no single provision). Read each instruction together with that wording to see what the change actually does. The context is not part of the change; where it is missing or incomplete, judge from the text alone and never guess the missing wording. Decide relevance by what the change does as the context shows it; the context alone, without a change to it, never makes the change relevant.

A change is **relevant** when its content could deliver the promise, deliver a real part of it, or contradict it or a part of it, judged against the promise: its title names the one commitment assessed, and its exact source wording, which can include neighbouring sentences for context, is what the party wrote. A change is **not relevant** when it only shares the general topic and changes nothing the promise speaks about.

- Read the full law-change text; never decide from its title alone.
- Do not make the promise broader or narrower than its title and wording; a neighbouring sentence in the wording is context, not part of the commitment.
- Do not judge whether the change is good, which party supports it, or whether it passed.
- When the text is not available and the title alone cannot settle it, answer relevant.
- **When in doubt, answer relevant.**

Give a one-sentence Czech reason in `reasonCz` that names what in the change does or does not concern the promise.

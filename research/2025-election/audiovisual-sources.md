## Czech Television caption leads

The official caption file for the 2 October Superdebate is:

`https://ctfs.ceskatelevize.cz/subtitles1746037296/7b/f9cc/7bf9cc8c-bcdc-4027-b7c8-2256c4c8020e.vtt`

The following passages are discovery leads. Captions can contain recognition, grammar, line-break, or speaker-boundary errors; verify them against the audio before preserving them as exact quotations.

### Petr Macinka, approximately 00:09:07–00:10:07

The answer describes an “ideal Czechia” that:

- has a balanced state budget and does not increase its debt;
- has exemptions from emissions allowances and the migration pact;
- uses the Czech koruna and places the national currency in the Constitution;
- does not have education “destroyed” by inclusion;
- does not impose high taxes on entrepreneurs;
- allows people to express opinions without fear.

This passage mixes outcome aspirations, policy instruments, and broad values. It must be compared with the final programme and split only where the original wording supports an assessable commitment.

### Tomio Okamura, approximately 00:10:16–00:11:20

The answer identifies SPD priorities conditional on entering government, including cheaper energy and food, accessible housing and healthcare, opposition to the migration pact, review of residence permits, higher pensions, cancellation of emissions allowances, and the absence of censorship. The caption wording around pensions is visibly ungrammatical and requires audio verification.

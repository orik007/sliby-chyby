# Leader portraits

These display assets were prepared from the official party pages listed in the corresponding `data/party-periods` record. They are visual presentation assets, not evidence sources for promise assessments.

- ANO 2011: official press-quality Andrej Babiš photographs, published at https://www.anobudelip.cz/cs/ke-stazeni
- SPD: official Tomio Okamura portrait, published at https://new.spd.cz/o-nas/predsednictvo-hnuti/
- Motoristé sobě: official Petr Macinka portrait, published at https://motoristesobe.cz/profil-petr-macinka

## Presentation backgrounds

The portrait backgrounds are accessible light UI tints derived from current official party visual materials. They are not claimed to be official brand specifications and are not used in promise assessment.

- ANO 2011: warm red tint `#F5A088`, derived from its current logo and graphic materials at https://www.anobudelip.cz/cs/ke-stazeni
- SPD: blue tint `#A7D4E5`, derived from its current logo materials at https://new.spd.cz/volby2025/ke-stazeni/
- Motoristé sobě: cyan-blue tint `#9ADDE8`, derived from its current visual materials at https://motoristesobe.cz/wp-content/uploads/Volebni_program-2025_MOTORISTE-SOBE.pdf
